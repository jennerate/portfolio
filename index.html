1 — META INTEGRITY

Building Meta's high-risk verification experience

2024 - 2026

What does it mean for someone to be an authentic advertiser, given variation of geographies, business types, and regulatory requirements? How do we prevent bad actors from bypassing our systems?

This work is an exploration of what it means to be an authentic business across the most heavily regulated corners of the internet, like gambling, prescription drugs, CBD, crypto, and addiction treatment centers.

2 — DESIGN SYSTEMS

Designing Meta's tile for global scale

2020

How do you create a card that can be enjoyed by everyone across differing language, device, and accessibility needs? How do we future proof a design to emerging trends?

This work is an exploration of what it means to design one of Meta’s most used components at a systems level, ensuring that every user has an equal experience today and tomorrow.
